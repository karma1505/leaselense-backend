# LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT (COMPLIANT)

This Agreement is made at Mumbai on this 1st day of February 2026.

**BETWEEN**

**Mr. Valid Landlord**, hereinafter referred to as the "**LICENSOR**" (which expression shall mean and include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the One Part.

**AND**

**Mr. Good Tenant**, hereinafter referred to as the "**LICENSEE**" (which expression shall mean and include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the Other Part.

**WHEREAS** the Licensor is the absolute owner of the premises described in the **Schedule of Property** hereunder written (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensed Premises").

**NOW IT IS AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:**

1.  **Grant of License**: The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee a revocable leave and license to occupy the Licensed Premises for **residential purposes only**. The Licensee acknowledges that this agreement does not create any tenancy or lease rights under the Transfer of Property Act.

2.  **Period**: The License shall be for a fixed period of **11 months**, commencing from 1st Feb 2026 and ending on 31st Dec 2026.

3.  **License Fee**: The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of **Rs. 20,000/- (Rupees Twenty Thousand Only)**. The fee shall be paid in advance by the 5th day of every calendar month.

4.  **Security Deposit**: The Licensee has paid a refundable interest-free security deposit of **Rs. 40,000/-**. This amount shall be refunded to the Licensee upon vacating the premises, after deducting any unpaid arrears for electricity, water, or damages.

5.  **Lock-in Period**: Both parties agree to a **Lock-in Period of 6 months**. During this period, neither party can terminate the agreement. If the Licensee terminates earlier, they are liable to pay the License Fee for the remainder of the Lock-in Period. _(Standard stability clause)_

6.  **Recurring Charges & Utilities**:
    * **Licensor's Liability**: Society Maintenance charges and Municipal Taxes.
    * **Licensee's Liability**: Electricity charges, Gas connection, Internet/Broadband, and Water charges (if metered separately).

7.  **Maintenance & Alterations**: The Licensor is responsible for major structural repairs. The Licensee shall maintain the premises in good tenantable condition (reasonable wear and tear excepted). The Licensee shall not make any structural alterations or permanent additions without written consent.

8.  **Inspection**: The Licensor has the right to inspect the premises at reasonable times, provided a **24-hour prior notice** is given to the Licensee. _(Respects TPA Section 108(c) - Peaceful Enjoyment)_

9.  **No Tenancy/Sub-letting**: The License is personal to the Licensee. The Licensee shall not sub-let, under-let, or part with possession of the premises to any third party.

10. **Police Verification**: The Licensee agrees to cooperate fully with the Licensor for mandatory Police Verification (Tenant Information Form) as required by local law.

11. **Termination**: After the Lock-in Period, either party may terminate this agreement by giving **one month's written notice**.

12. **Possession & Penalty**: On the expiry or earlier termination of this License, if the Licensee fails to vacate, they shall be liable to pay damages at **double the rate of the License Fee** per day, as per Section 24 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999.

13. **Registration**: This agreement shall be **registered** with the Sub-Registrar of Assurances. The Stamp Duty and Registration fees shall be borne by the Licensor and Licensee equally (50:50).
