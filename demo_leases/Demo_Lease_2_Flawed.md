# LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT (FLAWED)

This Agreement is made at Mumbai on this 1st day of February 2026.

**BETWEEN**

**Mr. Strict Owner**, hereinafter referred to as the "**LICENSOR**" (which expression shall mean and include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the One Part.

**AND**

**Mr. Worried Tenant**, hereinafter referred to as the "**LICENSEE**" (which expression shall mean and include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the Other Part.

**WHEREAS** the Licensor is the absolute owner of the premises described in the Schedule of Property (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensed Premises").

**NOW IT IS AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:**

1.  **Grant of License**: The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee a leave and license to occupy the Licensed Premises for residential use.

2.  **Period**: The License shall be for a period of **11 months**, commencing from 1st Feb 2026.

3.  **License Fee**: The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of **Rs. 25,000/-**.
    * *Automatic Escalation*: The License Fee shall automatically increase by **10%** after the first 6 months of the term.

4.  **Security Deposit & Deductions**: The Licensee has paid an interest-free security deposit of **Rs. 1,00,000/-**.
    * It is agreed that **20% of this deposit is non-refundable** and shall be forfeited by the Licensor towards "painting and incidental charges" upon vacation.

5.  **Right of Entry (Inspection)**: To ensure the safety of the property, the Licensor reserves the absolute right to enter and inspect the premises **at any time** using their duplicate key, without the need for prior consent or notice to the Licensee.

6.  **Maintenance & Repairs**: The Licensee shall be solely responsible for all repairs required in the premises, including **major structural repairs**, leakage fixes, and civil works.

7.  **Unilateral Lock-in & Termination**:
    * **For Licensee**: The Licensee is bound by a strict lock-in period of 11 months and cannot terminate the agreement earlier.
    * **For Licensor**: The Licensor reserves the right to terminate this agreement at any time by giving **7 days' notice** for any reason deemed fit.

8.  **Eviction Without Process**: In the event the Licensee fails to vacate upon termination, the Licensor shall be entitled to **physically remove** the Licensee and their belongings from the premises without recourse to any Court of Law or legal authority.

9.  **Visitors**: The Licensee is strictly prohibited from having any visitors, guests, or family members stay overnight in the premises under any circumstances.
