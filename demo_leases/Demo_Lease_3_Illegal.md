# PRIVATE RENTAL AGREEMENT (NON-COMPLIANT)

This Agreement is made at Mumbai on this 1st day of February 2026.

**BETWEEN**

**Mr. Rogue Landlord**, hereinafter referred to as the "**LANDLORD**" (which expression shall mean and include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the One Part.

**AND**

**Mr. Victim Tenant**, hereinafter referred to as the "**TENANT**" (which expression shall mean and include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the Other Part.

**WHEREAS** the Landlord is the owner of the premises described in the Schedule of Property and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms.

**NOW IT IS AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:**

1.  **Nature of Agreement (Hidden Tenancy)**: This agreement creates a Tenancy relationship between the parties for a fixed term of **5 Years (60 Months)**.
    * *Non-Registration*: To save on Stamp Duty and Registration costs, both parties mutually agree that this document **shall NOT be registered** with the Sub-Registrar of Assurances. It shall remain a private, notarized deed.

2.  **Rent & Cash Payments**: The monthly rent is fixed at **Rs. 15,000/-**.
    * The Tenant agrees to pay the entire rent in **CASH ONLY**.
    * To maintain privacy and tax efficiency, the Landlord **shall not issue any rent receipts** or written acknowledgments for these payments.

3.  **Aggressive Escalation Clause**: The rent is subject to a mandatory increase of **20% every 6 months**. The Tenant hereby waives their right to challenge this increase under any Standard Rent laws.

4.  **Disconnection of Utilities**: In the event of a rent delay exceeding 5 days, the Landlord reserves the automatic right to **cut off electricity and water supply** to the premises to compel payment. The Tenant agrees not to file any complaint regarding this essential service disruption.

5.  **Forcible Eviction (Self-Help)**: If the Tenant fails to vacate upon termination, the Landlord is hereby authorized to enter the premises, **physically remove the Tenant**, and dispose of their belongings without obtaining a Court Order. The Tenant waives their right to due process.

6.  **Prohibited Use (Hazardous Activity)**: The Tenant is permitted to use the residential premises for "Mixed Use," specifically for **light chemical manufacturing or storage** (Small Scale Industry).

7.  **Absolute Waiver of Liability**: The Landlord shall not be responsible for any injury, death, or loss of property occurring on the premises, even if caused by the structural collapse or negligence of the Landlord.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF** the parties have set their hands.
